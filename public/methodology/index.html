Verification methodology

How TerroirTrail verifies producer information

TerroirTrail is an independent discovery guide for people planning real visits to rural producers. Producer identity, public visitability, location confidence and road access are reviewed as different facts rather than collapsed into a single “verified” label.

Unknown does not mean false

If TerroirTrail cannot confirm a fact from suitable current evidence, the catalogue keeps that fact unknown or not publicly confirmed. Missing evidence is not converted into a negative claim, and a plausible inference is not converted into a positive one. Public opening hours do not automatically prove walk-ins, and a booking button does not automatically prove booking is mandatory.

Source hierarchy and producer identity

TerroirTrail prefers producer-controlled and authoritative public sources: official producer websites, official booking or visitor pages, official business profiles and published contact channels. Reliable institutional sources may add context. Third-party directories may help locate evidence but are not treated as sufficient proof for high-trust operational claims on their own.

Visitability, booking and walk-ins are separate

Records distinguish confirmed public visits, seasonal visits, appointment-only visits, uncertain access, not-publicly-confirmed access and unreviewed status. Booking requirements and walk-in status are stored separately and remain unknown when the evidence does not support a conclusion.

Location confidence

A verified entrance is different from a verified general location, and both are different from an unresolved point. A producer shop, visitor centre, estate and production site are also different public-point roles; one is not silently substituted for another.

Road access is reviewed separately from location

Valid coordinates do not establish that the approach road is suitable for a standard rental car. Road-access classifications are published only when supported by evidence. A map route can show network connectivity; it cannot by itself verify surface condition, width, gradient, seasonal condition or rental-car suitability.

Evidence freshness

Operational facts change. Where a visitability review date is available, TerroirTrail exposes it so travelers can distinguish reviewed information from timeless identity facts. Time-sensitive arrangements should still be confirmed directly with the producer.

Images, reviews and commercial relationships

Published imagery is supplementary discovery material and is not evidence of public visitability or safe access. A researched listing does not imply a commercial relationship with TerroirTrail, and commercial or affiliate relationships do not change editorial verification.

Corrections, opt-out and lifecycle

Producer facts may be corrected when stronger current evidence appears. Producers can request that a listing no longer be published. TerroirTrail avoids exposing stale operational details through traveler planning surfaces when a producer is no longer listed.

What the methodology does not claim

TerroirTrail is curated rather than exhaustive. It does not claim every producer in a region is listed, that every producer is open on a particular date, or that a road classification replaces normal travel judgment.
