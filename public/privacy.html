Privacy Notice

TerroirTrail Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 13, 2026 · Privacy contact: terroirtrail@gmail.com

1. What TerroirTrail is

TerroirTrail is currently a discovery-first guide for independent producers and rural projects. The launch product does not publicly offer TerroirTrail-operated tasting reservations, paid Explorer Pass sales, chauffeur bookings, or producer subscription plans.

2. Information used by the current product

  • Traveler accounts: authentication identifiers, name, email address, optional profile information, passport stamp IDs and personal tasting notes.
  • Saved favorites: favorites are stored in the browser on the current device. Signed-in favorites are separated by account on that device, but they are not currently cloud-synced.
  • Producer claims: a claimant may submit their name, account/email, selected estate and optional business evidence such as a tax/VAT identifier, registered business name, address or contact details. Submission does not itself grant host authority.
  • Public producer information: researched listing information such as producer/project name, location, contact details, visiting information and source-backed access metadata may be displayed publicly.
  • Location: if you use a browser or device location feature, location is requested for the user-initiated map action. TerroirTrail does not intentionally run background location tracking.

3. Why information is processed

Account information is used to authenticate users and provide account features such as Passport stamps and private notes. Producer-claim information is used to review whether a claimant is authorized to manage an estate profile. Technical information may also be processed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

The appropriate legal basis can depend on the specific processing activity and applicable law. This notice describes the product behavior; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

4. Services and storage

  • Firebase / Google: used for authentication, user-profile storage and hosting-related services.
  • Apple: may be used when Sign in with Apple is enabled and selected.
  • Supabase: used for the producer catalogue and related application data.
  • Map/tile providers: map tiles and geographic content can result in normal network requests to the configured mapping providers.
  • Browser local storage: used for device-local preferences and saved favorites.

5. Advertising

The application contains a Google AdSense slot. It renders only when an advertising slot is configured and available; otherwise it fails closed and no fallback sponsor claim is shown. When Google advertising is loaded, Google may receive technical information and may use cookies or similar technologies subject to its own policies and any consent requirements that apply in your region.

6. Sharing and public visibility

Private traveler notes and account data are not intended to be public. Public producer listings use business information that is researched from public or producer-controlled sources. A producer claim is reviewed separately from the public listing and does not automatically create a commercial partnership or public Experience.

7. Access, correction and deletion requests

You may contact TerroirTrail to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable law and any information that must be retained for legitimate legal or security reasons.

There is not yet an in-app account deletion control. Requests can be sent to terroirtrail@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as TerroirTrail adds or removes features, providers or commercial workflows. The date at the top identifies the current version.
