Privacy Notice

TerroirTrail Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 17, 2026 · Privacy contact: terroirtrail@gmail.com

1. What TerroirTrail is

TerroirTrail is an independent discovery platform for authentic producers, agritourism estates, wineries, breweries, distilleries, olive oil mills, and artisan food makers across Greece and Italy (Crete, Santorini, Peloponnese, Macedonia, Greece, and Tuscany). The public application is discovery-first; direct tasting bookings, consumer Explorer Pass purchases via Stripe, and third-party display advertising are currently dormant or inactive.

2. Information used by the current product

  • Traveler accounts: authentication identifiers, name, email address, optional profile information, passport stamp IDs and personal tasting notes.
  • Saved favorites: favorites are stored in the browser on the current device. Signed-in favorites are separated by account on that device.
  • First-party product-intent signals: narrow pseudonymous interaction events (such as viewing an estate, opening a regional guide, or requesting directions) used to evaluate maker popularity and guide quality. Raw event rows are retained for up to 180 days; non-identifying daily aggregates are retained for up to 24 months.
  • Producer claims: a claimant may submit their name, account/email, selected estate and optional business evidence such as a tax/VAT identifier, registered business name, address or contact details. Submission does not itself grant host authority.
  • Public producer information: researched listing information such as producer/project name, location, contact details, visiting information and source-backed access metadata may be displayed publicly.
  • Location: if you use a browser or device location feature, location is requested for the user-initiated map action. TerroirTrail does not intentionally run background location tracking.

3. Why information is processed

Account information is used to authenticate users and provide account features such as Passport stamps and private notes. Producer-claim information is used to review whether a claimant is authorized to manage an estate profile. Technical information may also be processed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

The appropriate legal basis can depend on the specific processing activity and applicable law. This notice describes the product behavior; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

4. Services and storage

  • Firebase / Google Cloud: used for authentication, private user-profile storage, operational records, and hosting-related services.
  • Apple: may be used when Sign in with Apple is enabled and selected.
  • Supabase: used for the authoritative public producer catalogue and geospatial data.
  • Map/tile providers: requests for map imagery or tiles (OpenStreetMap, CARTO, Esri) involve standard network communication (such as IP address) with the respective providers.
  • Google Places / Maps Media: for verified listings, place photo media may be fetched directly from Google Places API or linked to Google Maps.
  • Travel affiliate links (Travelpayouts): curated outbound links to third-party travel services (car rentals, ferries, transfers, connectivity). TerroirTrail transmits no account, personal profile, or booking data to affiliate partners, and sets no third-party tracking cookies. Pass-holders enjoy an ad-free experience without affiliate banners.
  • First-party intent telemetry: rotating session identifiers stored in browser session storage; for signed-in users, account identifiers are cryptographically pseudonymized using a server-side HMAC before storage. Raw analytics does not store the Firebase UID, private note text, precise movement coordinates, IP address, or outbound affiliate URL. TerroirTrail employs no third-party behavioral analytics or ad network tracking pixels.
  • Browser local storage: used for device-local preferences and saved favorites.

5. Advertising and payment infrastructure

Display advertising infrastructure (Google AdSense) and live consumer payment processing for digital passes (Stripe checkout) are currently disabled/dormant in this production release. No AdSense tracking scripts are executed, and no consumer credit card data is collected or processed on the public application.

6. Sharing and public visibility

Private traveler notes and account data are never public or shared with producers. Public producer listings use business information researched from public or producer-controlled sources. A producer claim is reviewed separately from the public listing and does not automatically create a commercial partnership or public Experience.

7. Access, export, and deletion requests

Authenticated users can access self-service account data export (downloading a machine-readable JSON archive) and self-service permanent account deletion directly within the in-app Account & privacy settings. The export includes retained account-linked intent events. Account deletion removes retained raw intent events linked to the account; historical non-identifying aggregate statistics remain.

You may also contact TerroirTrail to exercise any data protection rights (access, correction, erasure, objection, restriction, or portability) at terroirtrail@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as TerroirTrail adds or removes features, providers or commercial workflows. The date at the top identifies the current version.
