1. What TerroirTrail is

TerroirTrail is an independent discovery platform for authentic producers, agritourism estates, wineries, breweries, distilleries, olive oil mills, and artisan food makers across multiple European destinations. The public application remains discovery-first. Travelers may optionally purchase Explorer Pass convenience plans for ad-free planning and trip exports, while approved producer Hosts may separately purchase the optional TerroirTrail Partner annual subscription for eligibility for clearly labelled paid promotion. Direct tasting checkout and third-party display advertising remain dormant or inactive.

2. Information used by the current product

  • Traveler accounts: authentication identifiers, name, email address, optional profile information, passport stamp IDs and personal tasting notes.
  • My Trips and Explorer Pass: trip titles, optional dates, saved producer IDs, ordering/day assignments, and—where a paid convenience plan is purchased—plan type and entitlement expiry. Trip Pack files are generated at the traveler's request from saved trip data and current public catalogue facts; once downloaded, the local copy is controlled by the traveler and can become outdated.
  • Saved favorites: favorites are stored in the browser on the current device. Signed-in favorites are separated by account on that device.
  • First-party product-intent signals: narrow pseudonymous interaction events (such as viewing an estate, opening a regional guide, or requesting directions), together with coarse session-acquisition attribution such as Instagram/social or Google/search and a bounded campaign label supplied in a TerroirTrail link. Raw event and acquisition rows are retained for up to 180 days; non-identifying daily aggregates are retained for up to 24 months.
  • Producer claims: a claimant may submit their name, account/email, selected estate and optional business evidence such as a tax/VAT identifier, registered business name, address or contact details. Submission does not itself grant host authority.
  • Public producer information: researched listing information such as producer/project name, public business contact details, location, website, visiting information and source-backed access metadata may be displayed publicly. Sources may include official websites, public registers, mapping/business listings, producer-controlled public profiles or social pages, and information supplied by the producer or an authorized representative. TerroirTrail does not intentionally publish private contact details that are not presented for business or public use.
  • Location: if you use a browser or device location feature, location is requested for the user-initiated map action. TerroirTrail does not intentionally run background location tracking.

3. Why information is processed

Account information is used to authenticate users and provide account features such as Passport stamps and private notes. Producer-claim information is used to review whether a claimant is authorized to manage an estate profile. Technical information may also be processed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

  • Contract / steps at your request: account functionality, paid Explorer or Partner services, and other features you choose to use.
  • Consent: optional information or device permissions where consent is the appropriate basis.
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud/impersonation prevention, producer-ownership verification, moderation, service reliability, and maintaining an independent factual producer-discovery catalogue. Where public business information is personal data, those interests are balanced against the rights and reasonable expectations of the affected person.
  • Legal obligations: where records must be kept or disclosed to comply with applicable law.

Where personal data in a researched listing was not obtained directly from the person concerned, this notice identifies the categories and general sources used. Where GDPR Article 14 requires individual notice, TerroirTrail will provide it unless an applicable legal exception applies.

4. Services and storage

  • Firebase / Google Cloud: used for authentication, private user-profile storage, operational records, and hosting-related services.
  • Apple: may be used when Sign in with Apple is enabled and selected.
  • Supabase: used for the authoritative public producer catalogue and geospatial data.
  • Map/tile providers: requests for map imagery or tiles (OpenStreetMap, CARTO, Esri) involve standard network communication (such as IP address) with the respective providers.
  • Google Places / Maps Media: for verified listings, place photo media may be fetched directly from Google Places API or linked to Google Maps.
  • Travel affiliate links (Travelpayouts): curated outbound links to third-party travel services (car rentals, ferries, transfers, connectivity). TerroirTrail transmits no account, personal profile, or booking data to affiliate partners, and sets no third-party tracking cookies. Pass-holders enjoy an ad-free experience without affiliate banners.
  • First-party intent telemetry: rotating session identifiers stored in browser session storage; for signed-in users, account identifiers are cryptographically pseudonymized using a server-side HMAC before storage. Acquisition source and channel are reduced to an allowlisted coarse category before storage. Raw referrer URLs and arbitrary URL parameters are not persisted. Raw analytics does not store the Firebase UID, private note text, precise movement coordinates, IP address, or outbound affiliate URL. TerroirTrail employs no third-party behavioral analytics or ad network tracking pixels.
  • Browser local storage: used for device-local preferences and saved favorites.

5. Advertising and payment infrastructure

Display advertising infrastructure (Google AdSense) remains disabled/dormant. Verified Hosts may purchase the optional TerroirTrail Partner annual subscription, and travelers may purchase optional Explorer Pass convenience plans, through Stripe-hosted Checkout. Stripe receives and processes payment-card, billing-address and tax information directly; TerroirTrail does not receive full card numbers or CVCs. For Explorer Passes, TerroirTrail receives or stores the authenticated account identifier, Checkout/session identifiers, Pass identifier, selected plan, Stripe customer/subscription identifiers where applicable, payment/subscription status and entitlement expiry needed to provide the paid conveniences, prevent unauthorized access, support billing, and end access after expiry, refund or cancellation. Annual Explorer subscribers can manage billing through a dedicated Stripe Customer Portal.

6. Sharing and public visibility

Private traveler notes and account data are never public or shared with producers merely because they manage a listing. Public producer listings use business information researched from public or producer-controlled sources. A producer claim is reviewed separately from the public listing and does not automatically create a commercial partnership or public Experience.

Factual corrections, privacy objections and rights requests are free and do not require a producer claim or paid Partner status. A request not to be listed does not automatically remove accurate, lawfully obtained factual information, but TerroirTrail may restrict, correct or remove information where required by law or where continued publication creates a material rights, privacy, safety or legal concern.

Listing-correction and dispute correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to investigate the request, document the resulting decision, comply with legal obligations and prevent repeated abuse or conflicting changes. Source provenance may be retained while the relevant listing or factual claim remains active and for a reasonable audit period afterward.

7. Access, export, and deletion requests

Authenticated users can access self-service account data export (downloading a machine-readable JSON archive) and self-service permanent account deletion directly within the in-app Account & privacy settings. The export includes retained account-linked intent events. Account deletion removes retained raw intent events linked to the account; historical non-identifying aggregate statistics remain.

You may also contact TerroirTrail to exercise any data protection rights (access, correction, erasure, objection, restriction, or portability) at terroirtrail@gmail.com.

Where processing relies on legitimate interests, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may also lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority or another competent supervisory authority.
