GDPR & EU Privacy Notice

Privacy Notice & GDPR Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: September 10, 2026 · Data Controller: TerroirTrail (gian.fanourakis@gmail.com)

1. Introduction

TerroirTrail ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to safeguarding the privacy, personal data, and rights of culinary travelers, wine enthusiasts, and independent artisanal producers across Greece, Italy, and the European Union.

This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, handle, protect, and store your personal data in strict compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), the ePrivacy Directive, and applicable national laws.

2. Data We Collect

  • Traveler & Explorer Accounts: Full name, email address, avatar, authentication tokens (Google OAuth / Apple ID), favorite producers, and passport tasting notes.
  • Tasting Inquiries & Reservations: Visitor name, contact email, phone number, party size, and visit date/time preferences (shared directly with the estate host to confirm your visit).
  • Artisanal Producers: Estate name, contact email, cellar phone number, GPS coordinates, public operating hours, and photo verification.
  • Device & Geographic Data: Browser user agent, screen resolution, and temporary location when you tap "Locate Me" on the map (processed locally in your browser, never recorded in background).

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Contract Performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): To facilitate tastings, VIP Explorer Passes, and direct producer contact.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): For optional user account registration, Google AdSense non-essential cookies, and newsletter communications.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Platform security, bot defense, and verification of agricultural producer authenticity.

4. Cookies & Google AdSense Advertising

We use essential local storage to remember your authentication session and saved favorites. In partnership with Google AdSense, non-personalized or personalized advertising may be served to free-tier users.

In compliance with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2) and the EU User Consent Policy, visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland are presented with Google's certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) dialog, allowing you to freely grant, configure, or refuse cookie consent. VIP Explorer Passholders enjoy a completely ad-free, cookie-tracker-free experience.

5. Zero-Data-Selling Policy

We do not sell, monetize, or rent personal identifiable information to third-party data brokers.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data (Art. 15).
  • Rectify inaccurate records (Art. 16).
  • Request erasure of your data / "Right to Be Forgotten" (Art. 17).
  • Port your passport notes and bookmarked estates in JSON format (Art. 20).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

To exercise your privacy rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at: gian.fanourakis@gmail.com.
