# Phase 14 Validation Pack

This pack is for validating **Producer Insights (14.8)** and **Regional Intelligence (14.9)** before TerroirTrail builds or prices any paid product.

## Ground rules

- Show the working prototype, not a sales deck.
- Ask about decisions and workflows, not whether the dashboard "looks useful".
- Do not lead with pricing.
- Record requested workflows separately from requested vanity metrics.
- Do not describe TerroirTrail's regional catalogue as exhaustive.
- Do not imply that views, clicks, saves, directions or affiliate actions prove a visit, booking or purchase.

---

## A. Producer Insights validation — 14.8.4

### Who to interview

Start with 3–5 verified/known producers representing different operating models where possible:
- public walk-in producer;
- appointment-only producer;
- seasonal producer;
- producer with strong direct digital presence;
- producer with limited visitor information.

### Suggested first Crete cohort

Use a deliberately mixed operating set:

- **Baladinos & Sons** — cheese dairy; public visits; walk-ins accepted; booking not required.
- **Biolea Astrikas Estate** — olive mill; public visits; guided-tour booking required; walk-ins not accepted.
- **Douloufakis Winery** — winery; appointment-only; booking required; structured visitor hours; parking/language evidence present.
- **Cretan Brewery (Charma Beer)** — brewery; seasonal public access with published visitor/tour times.
- **Aerakis Cheese Products** — cheese dairy; visitability not publicly confirmed, useful for testing whether insights still have value when visitor operations are lightly documented.

This cohort intentionally spans public, appointment-only, seasonal and not-publicly-confirmed visit models. Treat participation as validation outreach, not partnership status.

### Show

Use the admin Producer Insights prototype for that producer:
- profile views;
- saves;
- trip additions;
- website clicks;
- phone actions;
- email actions;
- directions;
- previous equal-length reporting window;
- region/category context only when the sample threshold is met.

### Ask

1. Which of these numbers would you actually look at each month?
2. What decision could you make differently because of that number?
3. Is there a metric here that looks interesting but would not change anything you do?
4. Would knowing that travelers saved you or added you to trips change staffing, opening information, booking instructions or visitor communication?
5. Which outbound action matters most to you: website, phone, email or directions?
6. What information is missing that would help you act?
7. Would a recurring monthly summary be more useful than a dashboard?
8. Who in your business would use this information?
9. What would make you distrust these numbers?
10. What workflow would you want next after seeing a useful signal?

### Record

For each interview record:
- producer;
- date;
- role of interviewee;
- metrics they would act on;
- concrete operational decision mentioned;
- requested workflow;
- vanity-only metric requests;
- trust/confusion issues;
- preferred delivery format;
- follow-up needed.

### Success signal

Do **not** advance Producer Pro because someone says the dashboard is "nice". Advance only if multiple producers independently identify repeatable operational decisions or workflows that the insights would change.

## B. Regional Intelligence validation — 14.9.4

### Who to interview

Start with 3–5 relevant institutional users:
- municipal/regional tourism body;
- local development organization;
- producer association or chamber;
- geopark or heritage organization where relevant;
- destination-management or rural-development stakeholder.

### Show

Use one regional report and clearly separate:
1. **Supply/readiness** — audited catalogue coverage and evidence completeness.
2. **Traveler demand** — first-party intent observed within the selected reporting period.

Show the individual components. Do not present a single regional score.

### Ask

1. Which readiness gaps would matter enough for you to act on?
2. Would you use this for planning, producer outreach, grant/program design, visitor information, or monitoring?
3. Which metrics are missing for a real decision you currently make?
4. Is recurring monitoring valuable, or would a one-off audit be enough?
5. Would you need region-wide exhaustive coverage, or is a curated monitored sample useful if clearly labelled?
6. Which breakdowns matter: municipality, category, visitability model, season, access readiness?
7. Would you need an on-screen dashboard, scheduled report, export, API/embed, or presentation-ready output?
8. How often would the information need to refresh?
9. What evidence or methodology would you require before using the report externally?
10. What would make this materially more useful than data you already have?

### Record

For each interview record:
- organization;
- role;
- date;
- actual decision/workflow;
- metrics they need;
- required geographic granularity;
- required refresh cadence;
- preferred output;
- evidence/methodology requirements;
- whether curated/non-exhaustive coverage is acceptable;
- next step.

### Success signal

Do **not** build a B2B portal because stakeholders like the concept. Advance only if multiple users identify a recurring decision, monitoring need, audit need, or reporting workflow that TerroirTrail can support credibly.

## Validation decision table

| Signal | Producer Insights | Regional Intelligence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weak | Interesting but no changed decision | Interesting but duplicates existing information |
| Promising | Concrete repeated operational decision | Concrete planning/monitoring workflow |
| Strong | Multiple producers ask for the same workflow | Multiple institutions need similar recurring output |
| Do not build | Vanity metrics only | Requires exhaustive data TerroirTrail cannot honestly claim |

The next commercial step must follow observed workflow value, not the amount of dashboard functionality already built.
