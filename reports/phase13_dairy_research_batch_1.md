# Phase 13 — Greek Cheese & Dairy Expansion: Research Batch 1

Date: 2026-09-16

## Purpose

This is a research queue, not a publication list. No producer below becomes a public TerroirTrail pin merely because its identity or product range is confirmed.

Before publication each record must separately verify:

1. producer/business identity;
2. production-site location (not a retail shop, office, village centre, or reseller);
3. product claims and any PDO/PGI claim from an appropriate source;
4. public visitability independently from business existence;
5. road/access evidence independently from map location;
6. producer-controlled public contact channels;
7. Google Place identity if Google imagery will be used.

Unknown facts remain unknown. Exact coordinates must never be guessed from a village name.

## Batch rule

These candidates remain staged until the production-site pin is independently resolved. A producer website publishing a village/locality is enough to establish locality context, but **not** enough to manufacture entrance coordinates.

The first production import should favor records that can satisfy the full TerroirTrail Expansion Quality Bar rather than maximizing producer count.
