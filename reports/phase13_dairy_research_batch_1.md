# Phase 13 — Greek Cheese & Dairy Expansion: Final Shortlist

Date: 2026-09-16

## Purpose

This is the final producer-selection list for Phase 13 research. It is **not** a publication/import list.

No producer below becomes a public TerroirTrail pin merely because its identity, locality or product range is confirmed. Before publication each new record must separately verify:

1. producer/business identity;
2. production-site location (not a retail shop, office, village centre, or reseller);
3. product claims and any PDO/PGI claim from an appropriate source;
4. public visitability independently from business existence;
5. road/access evidence independently from map location;
6. producer-controlled public contact channels;
7. Google Place identity if Google imagery will be used.

Unknown facts remain unknown. Exact coordinates must never be guessed from a village name.

## Existing live dairy baseline — retain

The live catalogue currently contains two `cheese_dairy` records. They remain in the Phase 13 vertical:

1. **Aerakis Cheese Products** — Sokaras (Gortyna), Heraklion, Crete
2. **Tzourmpakis Dairy** — Mixorrouma (Agios Vasileios), Rethymno, Crete

This is deliberately not a list of the biggest dairy companies in Greece. The selection favours producer identity, place connection, family/cooperative continuity, regional cheese diversity and suitability for TerroirTrail's direct-discovery model.

## Reserves / not in the first final batch

These are credible producers but are not in the 16-record first shortlist:

- **Exarchos Cheese of Elassona** — strong family/PDO evidence; held as a reserve because Thessaly is not yet a distinct TerroirTrail destination and the first batch is being kept inside the current regional architecture.
- **Penga family dairy, Ano Poroia, Serres** — compelling recent family/own-herd story, but producer-controlled source and exact public contact/location evidence should be strengthened before inclusion.
- **Vigla Olympus** — real cheese producer, but current corporate ownership and larger industrial profile make it a weaker fit for the first producer-first TerroirTrail batch.
- **Kerkini Farm** — excellent family farm identity, but its official positioning is primarily buffalo meat rather than dairy; keep for a later farm/regional-produce phase unless its dairy line becomes independently substantial.
