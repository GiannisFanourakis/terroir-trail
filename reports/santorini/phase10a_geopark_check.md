# Phase 10A — Santorini Geopark Designation Check

Date: 2026-09-15

TerroirTrail's regional quality bar requires heritage/geopark claims to be verified rather than inferred from dramatic geology.

## Result

Santorini is **not** currently listed as a UNESCO Global Geopark.

UNESCO's current Greece list contains 10 UNESCO Global Geoparks:

- Chelmos Vouraikos
- Grevena - Kozani
- Kefalonia-Ithaca
- Lavreotiki
- Lesvos Island
- Meteora Pyli
- Nisyros
- Psiloritis
- Sitia
- Vikos - Aoos

Santorini does not appear on that official list. The island's volcanic landscape therefore must not be labelled a UNESCO Global Geopark simply because it is geologically significant.

Authoritative source checked:
https://www.unesco.org/en/iggp/list?fq%5Bsm_unsc_field_ref_countries_label%5D%5B0%5D=Greece

## Product consequence

No Santorini UNESCO Global Geopark record or badge is added in Phase 10A. Future local geological heritage features may be represented only with their actual verified designation and source, without implying UNESCO status.
